# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want tlgodraw to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for
everyone. All contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users are expected to
participate in good faith and help keep project discussions productive.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Treating people with respect and professionalism.
- Communicating clearly and assuming good intentions.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than individuals.
- Respecting different backgrounds, experiences, and points of view.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and working to correct them.
- Protecting private information and avoiding the disclosure of sensitive data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech, threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting, or persistently derailing discussions.
- Deliberate submission of malicious code, credentials, or harmful content.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including Issues, Pull
Requests, code reviews, discussions, commit messages, and project-related
communication. It also applies when someone is representing the project in a
public or community setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove or edit harmful content, close discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action based on
the severity and context of the behavior.

Maintainers will aim to handle reports fairly, promptly, and confidentially.
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not allowed.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel.
Please include:

- A description of what happened.
- Links, screenshots, logs, or other relevant context.
- The names or handles of people involved, if known.
- Any action you believe would help resolve the issue.

Please do not create a public Issue for sensitive reports. If there is an
immediate threat to personal safety, contact the appropriate local emergency
service first.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards and
is intended to support a respectful and inclusive project environment.
